## Company and Product Background

Tango Software is an ERP platform developed in Argentina by Axoft Argentina S.A. It is designed to optimize, automate, and centralize the integral management of companies across multiple industries and sizes.

The Tango ecosystem integrates administration, finance, sales, purchasing, stock, logistics, accounting, human resources, business intelligence, and artificial intelligence within a unified platform.

The solution is designed to adapt to local operational, tax, and regulatory requirements, offering tools for organizations that need operational efficiency, financial control, and technological scalability.

With more than three decades of evolution, Tango Software has consolidated its position as one of the most recognized business management solutions in the regional market.
